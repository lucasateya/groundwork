Privacy

Last updated October 1, 2026

Groundwork is a place to practice job and college interviews. Here's what it saves, where, and what it never keeps.

Without an account

Your progress is saved only in this browser, on this device: lesson stars, grades and skill scores, your quiz answers, your question-of-the-day result, and the days you practiced. Nothing is saved on our server. If you clear your browser's data, it's gone.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional and only for people 13 or older. If you sign in, we save a copy of the same progress to your account so it's safe and you can use it on other devices:

  • Lesson stars, grades and skill scores
  • Your quiz answers (for job and college suggestions)
  • Your question-of-the-day result and practice days

Google shares your name and email address with us so we know which progress is yours. Accounts and saved progress are stored with our database provider, Supabase. Signing out keeps your progress on your device. To delete your account and saved progress, contact us.

What we never save

  • Your interview or lesson conversations
  • Your voice

How the practice works

To write the interviewer's replies and your feedback, what you type or say in an interview, lesson or daily question is sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, when you send it. We don't save those conversations.

If you answer out loud, your browser turns your speech into text. In some browsers, like Chrome and Edge, that happens on the browser maker's servers. Groundwork never records or saves your voice.
